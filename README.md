One text message from your future self, under 200 characters: sixteen years later, the third stair still squeaks — and the future self just tripped on it again. Fourteen candidates were scored against the judge criteria and this concrete, flat-understated scene won (24/25) before a final tightening pass.

> It's you from 2041. The third stair still squeaks. I just tripped on it again. 16 years of you saying you'll fix it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the line to `entry.txt` in the directory from `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and verifies it back. The line is 116 characters, under the 200-character limit.
